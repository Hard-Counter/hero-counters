Hard Counter ("the app") is an unofficial companion tool for players of Marvel Rivals. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NetEase Games or Marvel.

## Information the app collects

The app does not ask for your name, email address or any account. Your settings (platform, rank bracket and role) are stored only on your device.

## Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

- device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, or the iPhone advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
- device and app information, such as model, operating system and app version
- how you interact with ads

Google explains how it uses this information here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Your choices

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under About, then Ad privacy choices.
- **iPhone:** the app asks whether it may track you. You can change this later in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's ad settings.
